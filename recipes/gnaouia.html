Gnaouia - Okra stew

Descripton

Gnaouia is a dish that I grew up eating it hales from my tunisian grandparents. It's basically a rich okra stew with chicken or your prefered meat and as my mom loves to say as all tunisien recipes start with oil and onions. It's flavorfull and filling and can be eaten on its own or with couscous.

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Oinons
  • Gombos/Okra
  • Garlic
  • tomato paste (this can be optional)

Spices

  • Paprikra
  • Turmeric
  • Salt
  • Garlic powder (if you don't have garlic)

Steps

  1. Add oil to a pot
  2. Let it heat up
  3. Dice the onions and put it in the pot
  4. Let the onions get clear
  5. Add the meat and let it golden on all sides
  6. tomato paste time
  7. Add the okras
  8. Add water
  9. Let cook
  10. Eat either as a stew or serve with couscous
