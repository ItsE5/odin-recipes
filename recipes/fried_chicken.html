Air Fryer Fried Chicken

Descripton

When I think feel good I think Fried Chicken. There's nothing quite like it one of the best ways to make chicken lately I've found that I enjoy any type of fried chicken. Lately my favorite one is air fryer fried chicken. I know it not technically fried in a vat of oil but it easy and still crunchy.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (any type thighs, legs or both)
  • Spice mix (with the spices below)
  • Baking powder
  • Corn starch
  • Flour
  • Optional oil spray

Spices

  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Chicken bouillion
  • Paprikra
  • Thyme
  • Black pepper
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Mix your spices together to create a spice mix. Once the mix is made rub it thoroughly on your clean chicken making sure it's nicely covered while saving a bit of the spice mix
  2. Rub some baking powder on the seasoned chicken
  3. Add flour and cornstarch to a bowl having more flour than cornstartch. To this add the rest of the spice mix.
  4. Add your chicken to the bowl and coat
  5. Spray oil on the chicken
  6. Put in the air fryer for 15- 20 mins at 180°c flipping and adding oil halfway
