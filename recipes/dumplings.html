Chicken Dumplings

Descripton

My sister's favorite food and thus the one I made the most. Also the one i've searched the most recipes of. My favorite recipe has been one I've found on youtube that i'll. link below. Chinese Chicken dumplings are so good and flavorful and is the perfect comfort food. You can either make the wrapping or buy some (it should save you time). Anything that's minced and I don't have I use powder.

Ingredients

Dumplings

  • Ground chicken
  • Minced Garlic
  • Egg
  • Sesame oil
  • Minced ginger
  • Soy sauce
  • Chinese chives (or onion substittute)
  • Wrappers or dough
  • Oil to pan fry them

sauce

  • Soy Sauce
  • Rice vinegar
  • Chinese chives chopped small
  • Sesame seeds
  • Siracha or a garlic hot sauce

    • Steps

    1. Mix the meat, garlic,egg, chives, soy sauce, sesame oil, and ginger in a large bowl until thoroughly combined.
    2. Place a dumpling wrapper on a lightly floured work surface and spoon about 1 tablespoon of filling in the middle.
    3. Wet the edge with a little water and crimp together forming small pleats to seal the dumpling. Repeat to form remaining dumplings.
    4. Heat 1 to 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place 8 to 10 dumplings in the pan and cook until browned, about 2 minutes per side.
    5. Pour in 1 cup of water; cover and cook until dumplings are tender and meat is cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    6. To make the dipping sauce: Combine soy sauce, rice vinegar, chives, sesame seeds, and chile-garlic sauce in a small bowl. Set aside.
    7. Serve and enjoy
